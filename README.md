# Superman (1978)

> "You'll believe a man can fly."

## Overview
* **Director:** Richard Donner
* **Genre:** Action / Sci-Fi / Superhero
* **Release Year:** 1978

## Main Characters
* *Clark Kent / Superman* - An alien raised on Earth who uses his superhuman abilities to protect humanity
* *Lois Lane* - An intrepid daily planet reporter and Clark's primary love interest
* *Lex Luthor* - A criminal mastermind plotting a massive real estate scheme

## Plot Summary
An alien orphan is sent from his dying planet Krypton to Earth, where he grows up to become the superhero defender of mankind while posing as a mild-mannered journalist..
